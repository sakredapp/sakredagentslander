Sakred CRM

Sakred CRM is a customer relationship management (CRM) and sales-automation application for licensed insurance agents. Agents sign in to manage the people they sell to and service — tracking clients and the policies they hold, working a sales pipeline from new lead to issued policy, calling and texting and emailing those clients, scheduling appointments, running marketing campaigns, and tracking the commissions and renewals they earn.

It is used by the licensed agents of Sakred Health, an insurance agency. It is not a consumer product and is not available to the general public — agents are given an account when they are contracted with the agency.

What the application does

Every function below is part of the application an agent signs in to.

Clients and policies

  • Client records — contact details, household members, and notes for every person the agent works with.
  • Policy tracking — the policies each client holds, carrier and plan details, effective dates, and renewal dates.
  • Documents — policy documents and forms stored against the client record.
  • Service requests — questions and issues raised by existing clients, tracked to resolution.

Sales pipeline

  • Lead management — new enquiries enter the system and are assigned to an agent.
  • Pipeline board — every lead sits in one of 31 stages, from first dial through appointments to application submitted and approved.
  • Appointment rounds — first, second and third appointments are tracked separately, each with its own outcome.
  • Reporting — activity and production reporting for agents and managers.

Calls

  • Outbound dialing — agents place calls to leads and clients from inside the application.
  • Inbound calls — calls back from clients are routed to the assigned agent.
  • Call recording and outcomes — where permitted and disclosed, calls are recorded and the outcome logged to the record.
  • Voicemail — pre-recorded voicemail can be left when a call is not answered.

Messaging

  • Text messaging — agents send and receive SMS with leads and clients; the full thread is kept on the record.
  • Email — agents send and receive email with leads and clients from their own address.
  • Shared inbox — calls, texts and email for one person appear together in a single thread.
  • Consent and opt-out — opt-out requests are honoured immediately and messaging respects the contact's local quiet hours.

Scheduling

  • Appointments — agents book, reschedule and cancel appointments with leads and clients.
  • Calendar — each agent has a calendar of their appointments, optionally synchronised with Google Calendar.
  • Booking pages — a personal booking link a client can use to choose a time.
  • Reminders — automatic reminders before an appointment, and follow-up when one is missed.

Commissions

  • Commission schedules — what each plan and product pays the agent, by level and age band.
  • Payout tracking — advanced versus as-earned commission, and when each payment is due.
  • Chargebacks and persistency — early policy lapses are flagged and unearned commission is reversed.
  • Statements and reports — earnings summaries and end-of-year tax reporting.

Marketing campaigns

  • Campaigns — agents and administrators group leads into campaigns by source and product, and message them as a group.
  • Message sequences — a scheduled series of follow-up messages sent over days or weeks until the person replies or opts out.
  • Source reporting — what each lead source cost and what it produced.

AI assistant

  • An assistant sends the first text message to a new lead, answers common questions about coverage, and asks the qualifying questions the agent would ask.
  • When the person is ready to speak to someone, the assistant offers times the agent is genuinely free and books the appointment on that agent's calendar.
  • It hands anything sensitive — a health question, a complaint, a request to stop — to a licensed human rather than answering it.
  • Agents can read every message it sent, and take over the conversation at any point.

Administration

  • Agent accounts — the agency creates, permissions and deactivates agent accounts.
  • Training — course modules agents complete inside the application.
  • Contracting — tracking each agent's carrier appointments and licensing status.
  • Compliance — business messaging registration, quiet-hours enforcement, opt-out handling, and use of the correct licensed business name per state.

Mobile

  • The same application is available to agents on iPhone and Android, published as Sakred CRM on the App Store and Google Play.

Why the application asks for Google account access

Connecting a Google account is optional. An agent can use Sakred CRM without one, and can disconnect it at any time from the application's settings. When an agent chooses to connect their Google account, Sakred CRM uses it only to operate the scheduling and email features that agent has turned on:

  • Google Calendar — read. To see when the agent is already busy, so the application only offers appointment times they are genuinely free.
  • Google Calendar — write. To create, update and cancel the appointments the application books with that agent's clients, on that agent's calendar.
  • Gmail — send. To send the agent's own follow-up email to their own clients, from their own address, so the client receives it from the person they are working with.
  • Gmail — read. Only for agents who turn on the email inbox feature, so the application can show their client email alongside the client record and draft replies that the agent reviews and edits before anything is sent.

Data from a connected Google account is used only to provide these features to the agent who connected it. It is never used to train any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model, and is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising or any other purpose.

Sakred CRM's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who uses it

Licensed insurance agents contracted with Sakred Health. Agents are given an account as part of being contracted; there is no public sign-up. Administrators at the agency manage accounts, permissions, commission schedules and campaigns for their agents.

Signing in

Agents sign in at www.sakredcrm.com/login. Information about becoming a Sakred agent is at www.sakredagents.com.
