Delete Your Account & Data

How to request deletion of your Sakred account and associated data

You can request that your Sakred account and its associated personal data be deleted at any time. Here's how.

Request deletion

Email us from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account", and include your full name and the email associated with your account.

Email a deletion request →

Or email team@sakredhealth.com directly.

Delete only some of your data (keep your account)

You don't have to close your account to remove data. Email the same address and tell us specifically what you'd like deleted (for example, particular records or contact details), and we'll remove it while keeping your account active.

What gets deleted

Upon verifying your request, we delete your account and the personal data associated with it — including your profile, login credentials, contact details, and account activity — from our active systems.

What may be retained

Some information may be retained where we are legally required to keep it — for example, records we must maintain to comply with insurance, financial, tax, or other regulatory and recordkeeping obligations, or to resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Any retained data is kept only as long as required and is then deleted. Data held on our behalf by service providers is deleted in accordance with our agreements with them.

Timeframe

We verify and process deletion requests within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity before completing the request to protect your account.

For more on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy. Questions? team@sakredhealth.com.
