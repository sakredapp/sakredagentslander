Support

Help with Sakred CRM & the Sakred Health app

Need help with your Sakred CRM account, the mobile app, or your client portal? We're here.

Email us

team@sakredhealth.com

We typically respond within one business day. Please include your name, the email on your account, and a short description of the issue (a screenshot helps).

Common questions

  • Can't sign in? Use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in screen, or email us and we'll reset it for you.
  • Need an account? Sakred CRM is for authorized Sakred Health agents and staff. Reach out to your manager or email us to be set up.
  • App issue or bug? Tell us your device and app version and what you were doing — we'll get on it.
  • Privacy & data? See our Privacy Policy or email us with any request about your information.
